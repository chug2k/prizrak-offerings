Going, Going

The annual railway auction of unclaimed luggage. Everything here was going somewhere.
Once a year, when the timetable thins and the left-luggage room can hold no more, the railway sells what nobody came back for. The hall smells of coal and wet wool. A porter tilts each case so the room can hear what shifts inside. You may not open anything before you own it. Sold as found. Sight unseen. The railway accepts no responsibility for the contents of other people's lives.
Conditions of sale  ·  six lots  ·  payment in korún, cash, tonight
Listen to the porter. The sound a case makes is the only honest catalogue.
What you do not win stays closed. Forever. That is the other price.
