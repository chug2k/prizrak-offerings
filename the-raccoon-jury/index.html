The Raccoon Jury.

Confess the thing you did — a meal, a text, a life choice, a thought you've been turning over — and a jury of five raccoons will put it on trial, deliberate through the trash, and return a verdict rendered entirely in raccoon logic. The court is in permanent session. Food is admissible as evidence. Grass is admissible. The moon is sometimes admissible.

The Verdicts

All verdicts are non-binding, nocturnal, and heavily biased toward anything wet or crinkly. The Raccoon Jury does not represent the legal profession. The Raccoon Jury does not represent raccoons. The Raccoon Jury mostly represents a small feral council that met behind a dumpster once and never disbanded.
