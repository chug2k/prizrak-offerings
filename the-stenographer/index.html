The Stenographer

She does not take down what you say. Ten thousand words cross her desk a night, and every one of them is an alibi. She takes down the pauses.

[ a small office. one lamp. the sound of a nib that never quite stops. ]

Three questions will be put to you. Answer however you like — the words are never kept, never shown, never sent anywhere. The page forgets them the moment you do. What is transcribed is everything else: where you stopped, for how long, what you took back. The parts of an answer that cannot be drafted.
