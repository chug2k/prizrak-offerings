Prízrak · a passage made blind

By Log & Lead

The fog has taken the sun, the shore, and the stars. What is left is what sailors had for four hundred years: a compass that lies a little, a knotted line for speed, a lump of tallowed lead for the bottom — and a chart that shows only where you believe you are.

Steer with the helm buttons (or ← →, hold Shift for 10°). Heave the log to learn your speed — it is the only speed your plot will trust. Cast the lead to feel the depth and the bottom; slow down and the cast steadies. The pencil line on the chart is your dead reckoning. The sea is under no obligation to agree. When you are certain, make landfall — and the fog will show you the truth in red ink.
