Prízrak · an affair of honour

Satisfaction

A duel fought entirely in courtesies, at dawn, at forty paces. Each politeness lands as obligation upon the man who must receive it. The first gentleman overcome by all he owes loses. Fatalities are rare, but gratitude has killed before.
The card arrived at breakfast, on good stock, in a hand so composed it could only mean violence. He demands satisfaction. The seconds have agreed on terms: no pistols, no sabres — manners. The surgeon will attend, because obligation, taken suddenly and in quantity, stops hearts.
