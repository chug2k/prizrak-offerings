The Understudy

Something has watched from the wings every night you played yourself. Give it one line. It will hand the line back — faithfully, at first.

[ the house goes quiet. a single mark on the boards. ]
you (as yourself)
press enter when the line is said
it is studying your hands, not your words
nothing leaves this page. the recording lives and dies with this tab.

click anywhere on the stage to hurry the night
[ house lights. the run is over. ]
what you gave it — night one

what it kept — closing night
